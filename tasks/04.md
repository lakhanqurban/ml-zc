## Q1: ROC AUC feature importance

**Question:** For each numerical variable, use it as score (aka prediction) and compute the AUC with the `y` variable as ground truth. Use the training dataset for that. If your AUC is < 0.5, invert this variable by putting "-" in front. Which numerical variable (among the following 4) has the highest AUC?

**Options:**

- **`lead_score`** ✓
- `number_of_courses_viewed`
- `interaction_count`
- `annual_income`

**Answer: lead_score**

AUC scores on training set:

- `lead_score`: **0.7882** (highest)

- `number_of_courses_viewed`: 0.7230
- `annual_income`: 0.6082

`lead_score` has the highest AUC among all numerical variables.

## Q6: Hyperparameter Tuning

**Question:** Use 5-Fold cross-validation to find the best parameter `C`. Iterate over `C` values: `[0.000001, 0.001, 1]`. Use `KFold(n_splits=5, shuffle=True, random_state=1)`. Compute mean ROC AUC and std (round to 3 decimal digits). Which `C` leads to the best mean ROC AUC?

**Options:**

- 0.000001
- **0.001** ✓
- 1

**Answer: 0.001**

Cross-validation results:

- C=0.000001: mean AUC = 0.617, std = 0.019
- **C=0.001: mean AUC = 0.749, std = 0.010** (best)
- C=1: mean AUC = 0.732, std = 0.007

`C=0.001` leads to the best mean ROC AUC of 0.749.
